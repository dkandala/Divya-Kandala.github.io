From Espresso to Eras: Analyzing Artist Growth Through Data

This project was a deep dive into the dynamic world where music, data, and fan engagement intersect. Using a powerful combination of Python libraries – Pandas for data manipulation, Seaborn for compelling visualizations, and BeautifulSoup for strategic web scraping. I embarked on a mission to uncover the hidden correlations in artist growth within the music industry. Ultimately, I analyzed over 15 years of music data, looking for answers to questions like: Do openers consistently see a spike in their streaming numbers after a major tour? Are certain genres more prone to this "tour opener effect"? My goal was to explore the intricate correlations in artist growth and understand the tangible impact of these high-profile touring opportunities.
