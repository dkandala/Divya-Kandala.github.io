Welcome to the Telugu Student Asociation Homepage

We are the Telugu Student Association and focus on sharing Telugu culture around CU to non-native speakers/native speakers. Our goal is to share Telugu culture and have a space for people to come and enjoy fun activites while learning more about Telugu culture.
Check out our events page to see what's going on!

What Is TSA?

The Telugu Student Association (TSA) is a student organization dedicated to promoting and celebrating Telugu culture, language, and heritage among students at the University of Colorado Boulder. We organize various cultural events, social gatherings, and educational activities to foster a sense of community among Telugu-speaking students and those interested in learning about Telugu traditions. Our mission is to create a welcoming environment where students can connect, share their experiences, and embrace the rich cultural heritage of the Telugu-speaking regions of India.

Do I Need To Be Telugu To Join?

No! While we are named the Telugu Student Association our goal is to spread Telugu culture around CU and allow for people to grow with us and learn more about our culture, while also providing a home for native speakers to embrace their heritage!
