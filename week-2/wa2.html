About Me

Growing up, I was always drawn to creating, through various mediums: drawing, painting, or hands-on projects. I took art classes outside of school and consistently chose art over other electives. My love for design became a defining part of who I am. While art was my starting point, I became increasingly intrigued by the intersection of creativity and technology. This led me to explore Information Science, where I discovered my passion for coding, front-end development, and user experience design. From crafting meeting posts as Marketing Chair of the Telugu Student Association to prototyping apps in Figma during my interaction design class, I found joy in blending aesthetics with functionality. Over time, I’ve expanded my skills to include Python programming, data ecosystems, and user research. I aspire to design intuitive, accessible, and impactful digital experiences that enhance how we interact with technology. With my aspirations, I am looking to pursue a role in being able to be a User Experience Designer/Researcher and further my goals and aspirations through this role.
