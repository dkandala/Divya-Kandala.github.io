Making an Impact at CU: Nobel Prize Monument Proposal

This project challenged my team to design a meaningful monument for the CU Boulder campus, focusing on both its physical representation and digital experience. Our core task involved creating a monument proposal that resonated with the university community, utilizing SketchUp and CAD for the architectural mock-up. Complementing this, we leveraged Figma to develop a website for the proposal, showcasing our expertise in typography and UI/UX design. Our proposed monument pays homage to CU Boulder's significant achievements in physics, specifically its multiple Nobel Prizes. As a team with a strong STEM background, we aimed to highlight this crucial aspect of CU's identity. The monument, strategically placed outside the College of Engineering and Applied Sciences in the main engineering quad, features a 3D rendered model of a Nobel Prize. This design incorporates materials corresponding to different elements, symbolically representing the diverse fields within STEM.
