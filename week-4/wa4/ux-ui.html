Man's Best Wingman

CU Boulder Spring 2024 | INFO 1121 Designing Interactions, Dr. Stephen Voida

How can we design a dating app that results in a better experience for all dog lovers?

To gather more findings into what our app should include and what users would expect and want to experience from Man’s Best Wingman, we conducted three different research methods: Interviews, Observations, and Surveys.

Goal
We were motivated to make a difference and a change in how dating culture and dating apps are integrated into society, an easier experience of being able to connect with someone and having at least one shared interest: dogs/pets. We wanted to be able to share this experience with everyone around and not go through all the jumps and hoops of normal dating apps, but already wanted a generic common interest among all users.
