2020 Election Data and It's Information Ecosystem

CU Boulder Fall 2024 | INFO 2131 Information Ecosystems, Dr. Leysia Palen

This project is about researching and analyzing data trends from the 2020 Presidential Election, mapping data journeys and social world models to examine complex filing structures. With that being said my group conducted secondary research to overall represent a final outcome and how that data got to the end result, using such tools like Miro Boards, secondary sources, and quantitative research methods.
