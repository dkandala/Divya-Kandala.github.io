The Information Workflow of CU's Student Ambassadors

Ever wondered how organizations really tick? My recent capstone project was an exciting journey into the "information work" of a real-world organization, the CU Student Ambassadors. Our goal was simple: to understand their current systems and identify ways to make them even better. Over several weeks, my team and I became embedded researchers. We started by conducting interviews with key members, gathering firsthand insights into their daily operations and how information moved (or sometimes got stuck!). This wasn't just about collecting data; it was about hearing the stories behind the workflows. We then dove into analyzing their information storage and flow, examining both physical records and digital platforms. This allowed us to spot patterns in how information spread within the organization and out to the wider community. We looked for everything from formal communication channels to informal ways information was shared. This project was a fantastic blend of discovery and problem-solving. By understanding their information ecosystem, we were able to pinpoint critical challenges and, most importantly, propose practical, data-backed solutions. It was a powerful reminder of how vital efficient information work is to any organization's success, and how data can illuminate the path to improvement.
